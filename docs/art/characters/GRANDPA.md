# Grandpa — fourth family member

Status: **EXPERIMENTAL — in production (started 2026-11-20).** Sequential rollout after grandma,
the father and the mother.

**His day.** Library and rest (GDD §5.1): wakes, eats, reads in the library's reading chairs
(08:00–12:00, 14:00–17:00, 18:00–19:00), rests in the living room (the olive armchair), sleeps.
Slow and observant: he notices unusual things well (GDD §5.4 — the most courageous of the family).
